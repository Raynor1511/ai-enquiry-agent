Harbourline Plumbing & Heating is a made-up company. Its enquiry agent is real: Claude reads your message, checks the business's services, prices and diary through its own tools, drafts a reply, and waits for a person to approve it. On this page, that person is you. If you approve, the reply is emailed to you.

  1. Send an enquiry below (or start from an example).

  3. Watch it being handled, step by step, with timing and cost. It takes about a minute.
  4. Approve, edit or reject the draft, as the business owner would in Slack.

What happens to what you send

  • Your enquiry is handled like a real one: it's saved, processed by Claude (Anthropic's API), and shown to the demo team in their private Slack workspace.
  • You get at most two emails: the confirm link, and the reply if you approve it. Nothing else, ever.
  • Your enquiry and everything the system made from it (records, draft, Slack card) are deleted automatically after 7 days, and calendar holds after 3. Unconfirmed submissions are deleted after a day. Copies of the emails we send you stay in the demo mailbox until it's tidied by hand.
  • Please don't include anything sensitive. Any name works.
  • If something looks wrong or you'd like it deleted sooner, reply to either email.
